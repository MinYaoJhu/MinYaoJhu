## 🔬 Research

- **Nodule organogenesis in legumes:** a 4D (space + time) Xenium atlas of *Medicago truncatula* root nodules, built with a 400+ gene plant Xenium panel, custom transcript relabeling and cell resegmentation
- **Haustorium development in parasitic plants:** how the stem parasite *Cuscuta campestris* (dodder) builds its haustorium, and how resistant tomato hosts defend themselves
- **Spatial omics in plants:** plant-compatible Xenium protocols, analysis workflows and training for the community
